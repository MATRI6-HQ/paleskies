About

India to
the World!

Paleskies builds and operates an AI platform that generates images and video for brands. The platform is the thing we make, sell and run. It is not work we perform on a brand's behalf.

The premise is deliberately narrow. Brands usually know what their work needs to look like; what they lack is a way to produce it at the volume their channels demand without handing the look over to a general-purpose model. Paleskies inverts that. The brand supplies the template: the framing, the composition, the pacing, the constraints. Generation happens inside it.

Today that generation runs on existing AI models we fine-tune for brand work, and the engine is yours to pick on any given run: the Paleskies in-house models, Seedance, Kling or Wan. Our own image and video models, built in India, are in active development. As they ship they join the same list as another engine you can select.

Platform

How it works.

01

Brand-defined templates

A template fixes what must not drift: framing, composition, pacing, type treatment, product handling. Generation happens inside those constraints, so images and video hold their shape across every run instead of varying one to the next.

02

Choose your engine

Model selection is yours, run by run: the Paleskies in-house models, Seedance, Kling or Wan. Generate the same template through different engines and keep the one that reads best. Our India-built models join the list as they ship.

03

PM & QA oversight

Images and video pass through a review layer before they reach a brand team. Project management and quality assurance are part of the platform, not a step bolted on at delivery.

Enterprise

Built for
brand teams.

01

Multi-tenant access

Each organisation works in its own tenant. Role-based permissions decide who can author templates, who can generate against them, and who can only review.

02

Invite-only onboarding

Accounts are provisioned by invitation rather than self-signup, so a brand controls exactly who inside it can produce work under its templates.

03

Audit logging

Generation runs, template changes and access events are written to an append-only log. Entries cannot be edited or deleted from within the product.

04

Dedicated onboarding

Enterprise accounts are set up with us directly, with templates built and validated against the brand's existing work before the first production run.
